fMRI analysis module: design hill climb

A Brainflow workspace that takes a preprocessed BIDS derivative to single-subject and group contrasts. Five rounds of mockups, each scored by four simulated critics. Where they disagreed we took the majority view, with Claude breaking ties. Every mockup is live: click the stages, contrast cells, HRF bases and filters.

Status Example data synthetic n-back study, 28 participants, fMRIPrep derivatives Engine Rust port of fmrihrf, fmridesign, fmrireg (planned)

Critic scores by round (0–10)

Stopping rule: stop when a round gains less than 1 point on average and no outstanding proposal wins 3 of 4 critics.

    Same stage stays open when you switch rounds,
    so you can compare a screen across rounds.
