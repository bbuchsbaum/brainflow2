# Segmentation module (UI)

Label tracing on a reference grid. The design is in
`docs/plans/segmentation-module-research.md` ("Recommended design: a Segment
mode built on six contracts"). There is no bridge section and no Rust crate
yet: editing is not implemented.

- **Stage: experimental, off by default.** Switched on, it contributes the
  palette command `segmentation.open` and the tool tab
  `segmentation.workspace`, which only says the module is in design. No menu
  item, pin, startup tab or shortcut. Add a bridge section (same id, stage and
  version) before any command reaches Rust.
- **Design summary:** [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) has the final direction H
  layout and states, the encoding rules, the Inspector, review and
  reconciliation, the score history and the open questions for an
  interactive prototype. Read it before implementing the UI.
- **Design exploration** (visual hill climb, rounds 0 to 7):
  - `components/directions/{a,b,c}/`: three round-0 directions (Workstation,
    Reading room, Protocol-guided). `directions/h/`: the round-1 hybrid (A's
    tiled shell, C's step card and landmark limits, B's start card, slice
    ruler and status chip). Each renders all seven design states
    (`components/shared/types.ts`, `DESIGN_STATES`). Later rounds edit only the
    chosen direction; fixes to shared rendering reach every direction.
  - `components/shared/`: pieces every direction uses: `SliceView` (SVG over
    the slice PNG; square pixels, nearest-neighbour voxels, partition
    labels, review encoding, brush footprint, crosshair clear box and edge
    ticks, tilted slab band, tick landmarks), `geometry.ts` (display/world
    mm, the brush rule "voxel centre inside the disc", the constraint gate,
    partition edges, review encoding, crosshair gap, native brush readout,
    Approve gate), `LabelList`, `Sparkline`, `ConstraintStrip`, proposal and
    review parts, `scenes.ts`/`views.ts` (per-state overlays), `mockData.ts`
    (including the SIMULATED native 0.40 x 0.40 / 2.0 mm geometry that
    direction H states; the imagery stays 1 mm and the disclosure says so).
  - `directions/h/workflow/` (round 5, direction H only): the workflow
    states `WORKFLOW_STATES` (AI prompts and the licence gate, lesions on
    FLAIR, contrast aids, history, save, resume, setup), rendered through
    `DIRECTIONS.h.workflow` from H's exported parts (`DirectionH.tsx`
    components, `hViews.ts` data). Their rules are pure functions in
    `shared/workflowRules.ts` (proposal gate, range brush and the > 50%
    partial-volume rule, 26-connected lesions, filter badge, per-label undo,
    colour candidates, label-volume header and table writers). DESIGN.md has a
    section per state. Round 6 adds `shared/session.ts`, the one mock
    session model (journal, kept branch, last save, per-slice provenance,
    ruler and matrix marks, provenance codes, recovered steps) every H
    screen reads, the `lesion-overview` variant, and the rules
    `overreach`, `nextUnseen`, `saveCheck`, `defaultConnectivity`, Fazekas
    grades and lesion types. Round 7 (final static pass) adds
    `slicesOutside`, the empty-label margin of `overreach`, `SliceView`'s
    `pxPerMm` (small multiples at another view's scale), band `alpha`/`edges`
    and `hatchPitch`; the AI state reads the session model at step 31.
  - `__design__/`: real MNI152NLin2009cAsym 1 mm T2w/T1w slices and MOCK
    subfield labels, generated by `tools/design/segmentation_assets.py`
    (inputs: the T1 from git, TemplateFlow T2w and HOSPA dseg). Regenerate
    rather than hand-edit. Round 5 adds a MOCK FLAIR with synthetic lesions
    (`lesionData.ts`, `assets/*_flair.png`, `assets/lesions.json`) from
    `tools/design/segmentation_flair_assets.py` (input: the T1 from git;
    round 6 adds `--t2` and the real T1w/T2w on the lesion slice,
    `assets/*_lesion.png`).
    Only the directions and the harness import it; the shipping tab must not.
  - Harness: `ui2/segmentation-harness.html?direction=a&state=tracing`.
    Capture: `e2e/playwright.segmentation-design.config.ts` writes
    `cruft/hillclimb/segmentation/<round>/<direction>/<state>.png` and a
    `manifest.json` (`SEG_DESIGN_BROWSER` selects the Chromium binary).
- **Contracts the mocks already follow** (keep them when the UI ships):
  labels in a layer are an exclusive partition; fills use one constant
  alpha (at most 0.25); every label strokes the inner edge of its own voxels
  over a thin dark halo, so both sides of a shared edge show in their own
  colours (review keeps rater A's edges once); while drawing the active
  label is 1.5 px full colour with a faint fill (0.10) inset by one voxel,
  other labels 1 px in their own muted tint (lightness and chroma, never
  alpha; muted pairs ΔE*ab ≥ 15; outer halo only), complete or locked
  labels a lighter tint, islands of the active label a neutral ring, and
  enclosed unlabelled gaps a neutral dot ("Outline only" drops the inset fill,
  F shows all fills); the crosshair is a solid hairline
  that opens a gap of at least three brush radii while a paint tool is
  active and (direction H) stays out of the edited structure's extent in
  every state, as edge ticks only in small tiles; a dash means rater B and
  nothing else (neighbour slices are a faint neutral line drawn only where
  they depart from the current slice, proposals a thin solid outline plus
  hatch, open ruler marks thin hollow bars, flagged review slices
  triangles); review draws
  rater A once (solid, label colour), rater B only where it departs
  (mid-grey dash), and disagreement as achromatic textures told apart by
  form (A only dots, B only dense hatch, relabelled crossed), never a
  proposal hatch; Return is the default and Approve waits until every
  flagged disagreement (a protocol landmark or at least `FLAG_MIN_VX`) was
  visited (direction H: every flagged region decided, Keep A / Take B /
  Edit); label volumes come from `LABEL_VOLUMES` only (labels complete
  mid-session equal the reviewed version); volumes show mm³ on a 1 mm grid and vx plus mm³
  otherwise; the brush preview is the exact voxel set the brush writes, drawn
  as a light fill with a 1 px neutral voxel-edge outline; locks never
  depend on visibility; a refused stroke says why once, at the cursor (plus
  a "Blocked" status chip); proposals use the target label's colour (thin
  solid outline, hatch) and a confirm button that names the label; proposal undo is separate
  from document undo; colour is data only (label swatches, images, semantic
  status), chrome is achromatic; the crosshair is green with a dark halo and
  the brush outline neutral white; labels rater B never traced are outlined
  only (no "A only" texture) with a "B: none" note; session facts (journal
  counts, provenance codes, ruler and matrix marks) come from
  `shared/session.ts` only; every proposal, AI included, is accepted slice
  by slice by default; lesions are outline only by default.
- **Tests:** `__tests__/` here (geometry, gate, scenes; `round1.test.tsx`
  for the partition renderer, crosshair gap, review encoding, Approve gate
  and native readouts; `round2.test.tsx` for the crosshair clear box and
  ticks, dash discipline, single-source volumes, per-slice bars, the
  flagged review gate and direction H's cuts; `round3.test.tsx` for
  inner-edge strokes, emphasis levels, gaps, neighbour departures, the
  per-label slice matrix, lock departures, review ordering and the ortho
  gutter; `round4.test.tsx` for tint muting and ΔE, islands, area flags
  and the one-scale matrix, the example style, active-only orthogonals and
  reconciliation; `round5.test.tsx` for the workflow states: proposals
  never take other labels' voxels, the filter badge, one lesion row per
  component, the range brush and partial-volume rule, per-label undo, colour
  candidates, intent 1002 and the table writers, sheets beside the primary,
  no dashes; `round6.test.tsx` for one session model across states,
  per-slice AI accept and the reviewed/unreviewed codes, no AI marks before
  first use, over-reach, plain-language records, the quiet save and draft
  warning, outline-only lesions, seed rows, types, span and mm³ bars,
  Fazekas, connectivity, setup identity and registration, the island key
  and the dock); the shell side
  (registration, stage, every direction x state and H's workflow states
  render) is in
  `../__tests__/segmentation.test.tsx`.
